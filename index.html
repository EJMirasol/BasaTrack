Common Questions

How do I sync my data?

Sign in with Google in the settings menu to securely back up your reading progress and streaks to the cloud.

Does it work offline?

Yes! BasaTrack is designed to be offline-first. Your progress is saved locally and syncs automatically when you're back online.
