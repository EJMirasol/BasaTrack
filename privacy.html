Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 12, 2026

BasaTrack is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains our data practices for the mobile application.

1. Information Collection

Personal Information: BasaTrack does not collect or require any personal information (such as your name or email address) to function.

App Data: We store your Bible reading progress, streaks, and preferences only on your local device. This data is not transmitted to any server.

2. How We Use Data

Your data is used solely to provide tracking functionality on your device. Since all data is stored locally, it is not used for any other purpose, and we do not have access to it.

3. Data Security & Storage

All data is stored securely on your device using Hive (local storage). You are responsible for your own data security. To back up your progress, you can use the "Export Progress" feature to copy your data to your clipboard and save it elsewhere.

4. Your Rights

Since all data is local, you have full control over it. You can delete all your data at any time by clearing the app's cache/data or uninstalling the application. No data remains on any cloud servers.

5. Contact Us

For any privacy questions, please contact: basatrackdev@gmail.com
